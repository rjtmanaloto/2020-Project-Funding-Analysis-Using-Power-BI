<h1>2020 Project Funding Analysis Using Power BI</h1>


<h2>Goal</h2>
Evaluate the distribution and efficiency of government-funded projects across the Philippines in the year 2020.
<br />
<br />

Example questions:
1. Present 2020 budget by City.
2. Identify top 3 most effective Senators at driving projects to "Completed" status in year 2020.
3. Compare 2020 Project budgets between Focus Area.
4. Visualize 2020 budget by Region.

<h2>Insights</h2>
<br />

1. The city of Pasig had the highest 2020 budget with P471 million and Manila had the lowest with P79 million.
2. The 3 most effective Senators with "Completed" projects in 2020 are Loren Legarda (9), Cynthia Villar (7), and Risa Hontiveros (7).
3. With regards to focus area, Health and Education had the highest 2020 budget with P506 million and Labor and Employment had the lowest with P95 million.
4. The region of NCR had the highest 2020 budget with P1,343 billion and Zamboanga Peninsula had the lowest with P119 million.

Overall, the analysis shows that with regards to the 2020 budget, the city of Pasig had the highest (P471 million) and Manila had the lowest (P79 million), the focus area of Health and Education had the highest (P506 million) and Labor and Employment had the lowest (P95 million), the region of NCR had the highest (P1,343 billion) and Zamboanga Peninsula had the lowest (P119 million). 
The most effective 2020 Senators are Loren Legarda who completed 9 projects, Cynthia Villar whp completed 7, and Risa Hontiveros who also competed 7.

Rurouni Kenshin DVD-BOX Special Ending has the highest rating/score, the dataset averages a 5.3 rating/score, number of episodes does not mean higher rating/score, certain studios (Studio Deen, Eiken, TMS Entertainment, Gallop, and Studio Pierrot) produce the highest rated/scored animes, and newer animes rated/scored slightly better than older ones.
